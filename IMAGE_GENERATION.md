# NGenWay Measure generated artwork

All seven assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool from the briefs in `IMAGE_PROMPTS.md` and saved in `public/images/`. The app already consumes these exact filenames. Both mascots use the same character design. All final files are square PNGs with transparent backgrounds.

The following are the exact generation prompts. The celebration pose uses `public/images/mascot.png` as its identity reference; its final background correction is recorded below.

## mascot.png

Saved path: `public/images/mascot.png`

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: NGenWay Measure web app header mascot, raster PNG displayed at 36x36 pixels.
Primary request: A cute, very simple mascot character built from a measuring tape. Create one complete centered character with friendly rounded eyes and a small smile. A rounded forest-green tape-measure case forms the body, with a broad mint-green face inset, small charcoal dot eyes and a friendly charcoal curved smile. A short warm amber measuring tape curls out of the side, with only a few chunky charcoal tick marks, never numbers. Simple short arms and little feet. Keep the form compact and instantly recognizable.
Style/medium: Flat vector-style illustration rendered as a PNG, minimalist, thick clean rounded charcoal outlines, broad flat solid fills.
Color palette: forest green #1f6f54, mint green #4cc9a0, warm amber #f4b942, optional tiny terracotta #e0663e details, charcoal #1c1c1a linework only.
Composition: Square 1:1 image, full character centered and filling about 86% of the frame, generous uncut contours, bold clean silhouette. Recognizable at 36px. One image and one pose.
Background: Genuinely transparent background with alpha, including the spaces between limbs. No background panel or baked checkerboard.
Constraints: No text, lettering, numbers, logos, watermark, gradients, shading, texture, drop shadow, ground shadow, background clutter, or extra objects. Friendly and approachable.
```

## Celebration background correction

The first celebration output contained a baked checkerboard. This edit used that output as its target; the corrected PNG replaces it at `public/images/mascot-celebrate.png`.

```text
Use case: background-extraction
Input image 1 is the edit target.
Remove the entire gray checkerboard background and all gray background scribbles from this image. Return a clean cutout of only the celebrating green measuring-tape mascot, the yellow tape it holds overhead, and the three colored stars. The output MUST be a genuinely transparent RGBA PNG with alpha=0 outside those subjects, including the enclosed gaps beneath the raised arms, above the body, between the legs, and around the stars. A checkerboard is NOT transparency; erase it. No white background either.
Preserve the mascot's identity, exact pose, rounded shapes, green and mint colors, yellow tape, orange side button, happy open smile, stars, charcoal outlines, full composition, and square canvas. Keep clean anti-aliased edges. Do not crop, add or redesign anything. Change only background to actual transparency. No shadow or gray outline.
```
