# NGenWay Measure — v0.1 Acceptance Criteria & Test Corpus

## Structural acceptance criteria (must hold for every input)
1. The raw entered measurement is always shown, unmodified, alongside its metric/imperial
   counterpart.
2. Output contains 2–3 comparisons (3 when available), from **distinct categories** —
   never two comparisons from the same category.
3. No comparison is ever shown with `ratio < 0.3` or `ratio > 10` for non-countable
   references, or `ratio > 150` (steps) / `ratio > 60` (walking-minutes) for countable ones.
4. Multipliers are displayed rounded per the copy rules in `MVP_SPEC.md` (never
   `1.47×`-style precision).
5. High-variability references (`variability: high`, or medium) get a copy qualifier
   (e.g. "car sizes vary") — see copy rules.
6. If fewer than 3 categories qualify at a given scale, show fewer (2 or 1) rather than
   forcing a disqualified or absurd comparison.
7. Comparisons are drawn only from `REFERENCE_DATA.md` — no invented/improvised objects.

## Worked example (fully hand-verified — use as the golden test)

**Input: 100 ft (30.48 m)**

Running the ranking formula in `MVP_SPEC.md` against `REFERENCE_DATA.md` by hand:

| candidate | ratio | ratioSimplicity | reliability | familiarity | score | category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| basketball_court_nba | 1.064 | 0.936 | 1.0 | 0.85 | **0.796** | standardized |
| sedan | 6.773 | 0.968 | 0.7 | 0.90 | **0.610** | vehicle |
| shipping_container | 5.00 | 1.000 | 1.0 | 0.40 | 0.400 | standardized (loses to basketball_court_nba) |
| walking_step | 40.64 | 0.984 (coarse set) | 0.4 | 0.75 | **0.295** | embodied |

**Expected output:**
- ≈ 1 basketball court (NBA)
- ≈ 7 car lengths *(car sizes vary)*
- ≈ 40 steps

This matches the flagship example used throughout the product brainstorm — confirms the
per-item familiarity fix (vs. category-level familiarity, which would have wrongly ranked
"≈5 shipping containers" first) produces the intended result. **Use this case as the
primary regression test.**

## Remaining 9-case corpus — pinned real output

Generated by running the actual v0.1 ranking engine (`src/core`), after fixing one bug the
first pass surfaced (see note below). This table **is** the regression baseline — see
`tests/rank.test.ts` for the enforced structural assertions.

| input | output |
|---|---|
| 6 in | ≈ 2 credit cards · ≈ 1 smartphone *(varies)* · ≈ 1 adult hand *(varies by person)* |
| 3 ft | ≈ 3 sheets of paper · ≈ ½ queen bed · ≈ ½ adult height *(varies by person)* |
| 10 ft | ≈ 1½ door heights · ≈ 2 adult heights *(varies by person)* · ≈ ½ car length *(sizes vary)* |
| 20 ft | ≈ 3 door heights · ≈ ½ school bus *(sizes vary)* · ≈ 4 adult heights *(varies by person)* |
| 50 ft | ≈ ½ basketball court (NBA) · ≈ 8 queen beds · ≈ 3 car lengths *(sizes vary)* |
| 100 ft | ≈ 1 basketball court (NBA) · ≈ 7 car lengths *(sizes vary)* · ≈ 41 steps *(varies by person)* — **golden case, see above** |
| 100 yd | ≈ 3 basketball courts (NBA) · ≈ 7 school buses *(sizes vary)* · ≈ 1 city block *(varies a lot by city)* |
| 500 ft | ≈ 5 basketball courts (NBA) · ≈ 7 semi-truck lengths *(sizes vary)* · ≈ 1½ city blocks *(varies a lot by city)* |
| 0.25 mi | ≈ 4 football fields · ≈ 5 minutes of walking *(varies by person)* — only 2 categories qualify, acceptable per criterion 6 |
| 1 mi | ≈ 20 minutes of walking *(varies by person)* — only 1 category qualifies, acceptable per criterion 6; confirms the known thin-coverage gap flagged in `REFERENCE_DATA.md` for ≥0.25mi |

**Bug found and fixed during this run:** the first pass showed `door_height` (2.03m) and
`queen_bed` (2.03m) together at 10 ft / 20 ft — technically distinct categories, but
physically near-identical lengths, so the second comparison added no new information. Fixed
by adding a length-diversity rule to `rankComparisons` in `src/core/rank.ts`: skip a
candidate whose `canonicalLength` is within 10% of an already-picked candidate's, regardless
of category. This is also what quietly dropped `city_block` (100m) in favor of the
higher-scoring `football_field` (109.7m) at 0.25 mi.

**Known-thin-coverage gap confirmed as real, not just anticipated:** 0.25mi and 1mi both
return fewer than 3 comparisons because the seed dataset has no standardized reference
between `football_field`/`basketball_court` (~28–110m) and nothing large enough for
1mi-scale beyond `walking_minute` and `city_block`. Acceptable for v0.1 per `PRODUCT.md`;
first candidate to revisit if user testing shows 0.25mi+ inputs are common.
